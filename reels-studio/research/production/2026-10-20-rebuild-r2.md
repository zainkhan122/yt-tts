# Rebuilds r2 — 2026-10-20

What this is: the four most recent Shorts re-rendered with the catch-scene fix
(dim from frame one, instant evidence ring, one-card evidence walk, caption-fit)
and the honest rebuild verification (published audio reused bit-for-bit).

Source of truth: release `renders-2026-10-20-r2`. Each entry lists the render run,
the artifact checks, and the measured before/after.

Measured with the same method on both files: `ffmpeg -ss <t> -i <file> -frames:v 1
-vf crop=1080:1000:0:120` then mean luma of that crop (the media band, captions
excluded). 0-255 scale.
