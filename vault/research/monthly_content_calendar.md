# 📅 MONTH 1 CONTENT CALENDAR: Rare Personality Types + Jungian Psychology
## Theme: "You're Not Broken — You're Built Differently"
### Weeks 1-4 | 12 Videos | 3 Per Week (Mon/Wed/Fri)

---

## WEEK 1: THE VALIDATION PHASE — "It's Not Just You"
*Goal: Make viewers feel SEEN. These are high-identity-confirmation videos that drive early subscribes.*

### Video 1: Monday
**Title:** The Psychology of People Who Feel Everything Too Deeply
**Pain Point:** Core Pain #4 (Emotional Flooding) + #1 (Chronic Misunderstanding)
**Jungian Concept:** Heightened affective empathy / unintegrated Fe
**Angle:** Most content says "being an empath is a gift." This video says: "It's exhausting, here's WHY your nervous system does this, and here's how to build boundaries without becoming cold."
**Hook:** "You walk into a room and immediately know who's fighting, who's hurting, who's lying. You didn't ask for this. Here's what's actually happening in your brain."

---

### Video 2: Wednesday
**Title:** Why Smart People Feel Chronically Misunderstood
**Pain Point:** Core Pain #1 + #3 (Rarity Burden)
**Jungian Concept:** Ni-dominant cognition (INFJ/INTJ) vs. Si-dominant majority
**Angle:** This isn't about IQ. It's about cognitive STYLE. When your brain processes through abstract pattern-matching (Ni) and most people process through concrete experience (Si), you're literally speaking different languages.
**Hook:** "Have you ever explained something you KNOW is true, only to watch their eyes glaze over? You're not bad at communicating. You're using a different operating system."

---

### Video 3: Friday
**Title:** The Psychology of People Who Go Quiet When They're Hurt
**Pain Point:** Core Pain #4 (Door Slam / Emotional Shutdown)
**Jungian Concept:** Shadow repression / Fe-Ti axis conflict
**Angle:** Why rare types withdraw instead of fighting back. It's not weakness — it's that your Fe (harmony drive) overrides your Fi (self-advocacy). You'd rather disappear than cause conflict.
**Hook:** "You've been hurt. But instead of yelling, instead of fighting, you just... go silent. And people mistake your silence for not caring. Here's what's really happening."

---

## WEEK 2: THE FRAMEWORK PHASE — "Here's WHY" 
*Goal: Introduce Jungian concepts as explanatory tools. The viewer now has words for their experience.*

### Video 4: Monday
**Title:** The Loneliest Personality Types (And Why They Can't Find Each Other)
**Pain Point:** Core Pain #2 (Loneliness Paradox) + #6 (Authenticity Hunger)
**Jungian Concept:** The paradox of individuation — becoming yourself means losing the collective
**Angle:** INFJs, INTJs, INFPs, INTPs are all looking for each other. But each type's defense mechanisms make them invisible to the others. The INFJ hides behind a social mask. The INTJ hides behind achievement. The INFP hides behind art. They walk past each other every day.
**Hook:** "There are millions of people exactly like you. You've probably walked past three of them this week. Here's why you didn't recognize each other."

---

### Video 5: Wednesday
**Title:** Carl Jung's Darkest Insight (And Why It Changes Everything)
**Pain Point:** Core Pain #5 (Shadow / Unlived Self)
**Jungian Concept:** The Shadow — not your darkness, but your UNLIVED life
**Angle:** Most people think the shadow is about suppressed evil. Jung actually meant something different: the shadow contains everything you COULD have been but weren't allowed to be. The confident you. The angry you. The free you. Integration isn't about controlling darkness — it's about recovering wholeness.
**Hook:** "Carl Jung said something terrifying: the person you think you are is only half of who you actually are. The other half? You buried it before you turned 12."

---

### Video 6: Friday
**Title:** Why Your Brain Won't Stop Overthinking at 3AM
**Pain Point:** Core Pain #3 (Rarity Burden) + rumination
**Jungian Concept:** The Ni-Ti Loop — when intuition and logic trap each other
**Angle:** Specifically for INFJs and INTJs. The Ni-Ti loop: you withdraw from the external world (abandoning Fe/Te), then your intuition and logic spiral endlessly with no resolution. This isn't "anxiety" — it's a cognitive function imbalance with a specific exit strategy.
**Hook:** "3AM. Everyone's asleep. And your brain is running through a conversation from 2016, an email you need to send, and the meaning of existence. All at once. This isn't insomnia. It's a cognitive loop — and there's a way out."

---

## WEEK 3: THE SHADOW PHASE — "Face What You've Buried"
*Goal: Challenge the viewer. Move from validation to transformation. These videos drive comments and shares.*

### Video 7: Monday
**Title:** The Psychology of People Who Are "Too Much"
**Pain Point:** Core Pain #7 (Intensity Problem)
**Jungian Concept:** Unintegrated intensity as shadow gold
**Angle:** You've been told you're too intense, too serious, too deep, too sensitive. But "too much" for the wrong people means "exactly right" for the right ones. The problem isn't your intensity — it's that you've been calibrating yourself for people who can't handle depth.
**Hook:** "How many times have you heard 'you're just too intense'? How many times have you apologized for it? What if your intensity isn't a flaw — what if it's the clearest signal you have about who belongs in your life?"

---

### Video 8: Wednesday
**Title:** Why You're Attracted to People Who Can't Love You
**Pain Point:** Core Pain #5 (Shadow as relational pattern)
**Jungian Concept:** Anima/Animus projection — you're not attracted to THEM, you're attracted to your own unintegrated qualities
**Angle:** Jung said we fall in love with people who carry the parts of ourselves we can't access. INFJs fall for emotionally unavailable people because emotional availability is what they deny themselves. INTJs fall for chaotic people because chaos is what their controlled life lacks. The pattern isn't "bad luck" — it's projection.
**Hook:** "You keep falling for the same person in a different body. They're all emotionally unavailable. All mysterious. All just out of reach. What if the person you're actually trying to reach... is a version of yourself you've locked away?"

---

### Video 9: Friday
**Title:** The Psychology of People Who Disappear Without Warning
**Pain Point:** Core Pain #4 (The Door Slam)
**Jungian Concept:** Shadow possession / the autonomous complex
**Angle:** The INFJ "door slam" — cutting someone off completely and irrevocably. But Jung would say: when the shadow takes over, you become the very thing you fear. The door slam isn't strength — it's the shadow running the show. Real strength is staying present while maintaining boundaries.
**Hook:** "One day you're in their life. The next day, you never existed. No fight. No explanation. Just silence. If you've been on either side of this — here's what Jung would say is really happening."

---

## WEEK 4: THE INTEGRATION PHASE — "Becoming Whole"
*Goal: Practical application. Give the viewer tools. These videos drive retention and return visits.*

### Video 10: Monday
**Title:** What Jung Knew About Loneliness That We've Forgotten
**Pain Point:** Core Pain #2 (Loneliness Paradox) + #5 (Individuation)
**Jungian Concept:** Individuation as the loneliest journey — and the only one worth taking
**Angle:** Jung himself spent 6 years in near-total isolation after his break with Freud. He wasn't hiding. He was individuating — having conversations with his own unconscious that produced the Red Book. Modern psychology sells "connection" as the goal. Jung saw loneliness as the unavoidable price of becoming a self.
**Hook:** "Carl Jung, one of the greatest minds in psychology, spent 6 years almost completely alone. Talking to voices in his head. Drawing his dreams. His colleagues thought he'd gone mad. He called it the most important thing he ever did."

---

### Video 11: Wednesday
**Title:** The Psychology of People Who See What Others Don't
**Pain Point:** Core Pain #1 + #3 (Misunderstanding + Rarity)
**Jungian Concept:** Ni as the "prophetic" function — and the price of seeing clearly
**Angle:** This was Apex Psychology's 862K-view hit — and for good reason. But I'd go DEEPER. Connect it to Jung's concept of the collective unconscious as the well you're drawing from. Your "intuition" isn't magic — it's access to a deeper layer of pattern recognition that most people have never developed.
**Hook:** "You notice the micro-expression before the lie. You feel the tension before the argument. You predict the breakup before the couple knows it's coming. People call it intuition. Jung called it something else entirely."

---

### Video 12: Friday
**Title:** The 4 Jungian Archetypes You've Been Living Without Knowing It
**Pain Point:** All core pains — integration video
**Jungian Concept:** The Self, Shadow, Anima/Animus, Persona
**Angle:** A beginner-friendly but deep introduction to Jung's core archetypes — framed as "the four people living inside you." This is an evergreen foundation video that you'll link back to in future content. High shareability.
**Hook:** "There are four people living inside you right now. You only know one of them — and even that one, you don't know as well as you think. The other three? They've been running your life from the shadows."
