## PART 3: THE BOTTLENECK YOU CAN'T IGNORE

The tool stack is solved. The research methodology is solved. The content calendar is built.

Here's what will determine whether this channel succeeds or fails:

### 1. Script Differentiation
Google AI Studio will give you a solid first draft. But the algorithm knows when 100 channels are publishing the same AI-generated script. You MUST inject original perspective. This means:
- Read the AI script aloud. Does any sentence make you cringe? Cut it.
- Add one personal observation or unique metaphor per script. Something only YOU would say.
- The AI can explain Jung. But it can't describe what it feels like when you door-slam someone. That's you.

### 2. Thumbnail Packaging
This is worth saying bluntly: a mediocre script with a GREAT thumbnail + title will outperform a brilliant script with a bad thumbnail. Every. Single. Time. Spend 25% of your production time on thumbnails. Minimum.

### 3. The First 90 Days
The psychology niche's "valley of death" is brutal. You'll publish 36+ videos before seeing real traction. Most people quit at video 15. The ones who make it are the ones who keep publishing when the views are in single digits. There's no shortcut for this.
