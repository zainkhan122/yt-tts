# Video 07 — Déjà Vu: When Familiarity Arrives Before Memory

Viewer experience: a new room suddenly feels remembered, with a powerful but unreliable sense of certainty.
Mechanism: familiarity signal, recollection mismatch, memory monitoring and prediction error.
Guardrails: no past-life claims, no diagnosis, no claim that déjà vu proves prediction; distinguish healthy everyday déjà vu from neurological conditions.
