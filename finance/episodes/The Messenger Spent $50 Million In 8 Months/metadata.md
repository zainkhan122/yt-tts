## DESCRIPTION
The Messenger spent $50 million in 8 months.

Founded in West Palm Beach by Jimmy Finkelstein after he sold The Hill, JAF Communications launched the free news site on 15 May 2023 with tens of millions from named investors. The plan was a national, down-the-middle newsroom: on the order of 150–175 journalists at launch, about 550 within a year, 100 million monthly readers, $100 million of revenue by 2024. Advertising would pay for it.

About 300 people were hired. Near the end of 2023, revenue was about $3 million. The New York Times, via TheWrap, reported more than $38 million burned and about $1.8 million cash. Comscore put unique visitors around 11 million in October 2023 against a 100 million poster. On 31 January 2024 Finkelstein emailed staff: the raise had failed, the site was done that day. The Times called it one of the biggest busts in online news.
