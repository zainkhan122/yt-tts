## Long-form one-pagers

### V01 — Why Everything Changes After $20,000
- **Formula:** Why…  
- **Keyword:** $20,000 / net worth  
- **Mechanism:** first investable surplus — cash that is no longer survival money.  
- **Charts:** ladder $0 → $5k → $20k → $100k; “what $20k *does*” (3 months expenses vs. invested).  
- **Source spine:** Fed Survey of Consumer Finances (median US transaction-account + net worth by age); BLS CPI for “why cash melts.” Exact figures go in `v01/sources.md` at script time.  
- **Thumb:** THE FIRST THRESHOLD (big $20,000 stat card).  
- **Hook:** “Twenty thousand dollars is not rich. It is the first time the math starts working for you instead of on you.”  
- **Midpoint:** someone who hit $20k and immediately bought a car — the threshold reset to zero.  
- **Closer:** “Was your first twenty thousand a threshold — or a purchase?”

### V02 — Compound Interest Doesn't Make Most People Rich
- **Formula:** Claim  
- **Keyword:** compound interest  
- **Mechanism:** at low balances, **savings rate** dwarfs return rate. 8% of almost-nothing is nothing.  
- **Charts:** two lines — 8% on $0 contributions vs 0% with monthly additions; stacked “rate vs deposits.”  
- **Thumb:** THE LIE OF 8%  
- **Hook:** “Compound interest is not why most people stay poor. It is why posters stay pretty.”  
- **Closer:** “If you could only keep one number this year — the rate, or the amount you actually put in — which would you pick?”

### V03 — 3 Net Worth Numbers That Matter Before 40
- **Formula:** List  
- **Keyword:** net worth  
- **Mechanism:** solvency (NW > 0), buffer (3 months), optionality (≈1× gross salary). Salary is a flow; these are stocks.  
- **Charts:** three gauges; salary vs net worth scatter (illustrative).  
- **Thumb:** NOT YOUR SALARY  
- **Closer:** “Which of the three numbers are you actually tracking?”

### V04 — Why Two Incomes Made Families Poorer
- **Formula:** Why…  
- **Keyword:** two incomes  
- **Mechanism:** Elizabeth Warren-style two-income trap — housing/childcare/transport bid up to eat the second paycheck; volatility of two job-dependent fixed costs.  
- **Charts:** 1970s one-income fixed-cost stack vs today two-income stack (sourced, labeled as illustration + book/data).  
- **Thumb:** THE SECOND PAYCHECK  
- **Closer:** “If one income vanished next month, which costs are actually optional?”

### V05 — If I Started From $0 in 2026, I'd Do This
- **Formula:** If I…  
- **Keyword:** started from $0  
- **Mechanism:** order of operations — buffer, high-cost debt, then broad low-cost funds. **No tickers.**  
- **Charts:** flowchart (the graphic *is* the video).  
- **Thumb:** SKIP THE TIPS  
- **Closer:** “If you had to start Monday with zero, which step would you skip — and why?”

### V06 — Why Too Much Cash in the Bank Is a Trap
- **Formula:** Why…  
- **Keyword:** cash in the bank  
- **Mechanism:** inflation as a silent fee on excess cash above a buffer.  
- **Charts:** $10k cash vs CPI-adjusted real value over 10 years (BLS CPI).  
- **Thumb:** CASH IS A FEE  
- **Closer:** “How many months of expenses is in your account right now — and how many extra years of fee on top?”

### V07 — 7 Signs Someone Is Secretly Wealthy
- **Formula:** List  
- **Keyword:** secretly wealthy  
- **Mechanism:** consumption is a poor proxy for the balance sheet. Quiet math: time, optionality, boring assets.  
- **Charts:** “looks rich vs is rich” 2×2.  
- **Thumb:** QUIET MATH  
- **Closer:** “Which sign did you mistake for wealth — and which one do you actually want?”

### V08 — Once You Hit $100K, Net Worth Starts Compounding
- **Formula:** Once you…  
- **Keyword:** $100K / net worth  
- **Mechanism:** critical mass — a 7% year on $100k ($7k) starts to rival a year’s new savings for many households.  
- **Charts:** slope change at $100k; Humphrey/Nischa packaging, our numbers sourced.  
- **Thumb:** THE SLOPE CHANGES  
- **Closer:** “What would have to be true for your slope to change this year?”
