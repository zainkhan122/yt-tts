1 · Create the accounts in this order

  1. Brand Gmail: one new Gmail used for every account below. Turn on 2-step verification. Save all logins in a password manager.
  2. YouTube: sign in with the brand Gmail, create the channel 'Hypeless', claim the handle @hypeless.
  3. Facebook Page: from your personal Facebook, create the Page 'Hypeless' (Meta requires a personal admin; it stays private).
  4. Instagram: sign up as hypeless (brand Gmail), switch to a Professional (Creator) account, then link it to the Facebook Page.
  5. TikTok: sign up as hypeless (brand Gmail), then switch to a Business account.
  6. X: sign up as hypeless (brand Gmail).
  7. Same handle everywhere. If hypeless is taken on any platform, use hypelessai on ALL of them.

Settings
  • Settings > Channel > Basic info: Country = Pakistan (your real country; YouTube Partner Program payouts and tax use it).
  • Settings > Channel > Advanced settings: audience = 'No, set this channel as not made for kids'.
  • Settings > Upload defaults: Category = Science & Technology, Video language = English, Comments = 'Hold potentially inappropriate comments for review'.
  • Verify your phone number (Settings > Channel > Feature eligibility) to unlock custom thumbnails, longer videos and links.
  • Per video, 'Altered or synthetic content' = No. A generic AI narrator over real screen footage needs no label under YouTube's rules. Answer Yes only if a video shows realistic AI-made people or events, or uses AI-generated music.
