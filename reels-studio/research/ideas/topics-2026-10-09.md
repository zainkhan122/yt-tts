## Why the system kept picking repos (diagnosis)
1. `trend_radar.py` ranks by GitHub star velocity, so repos always top the list.
2. `idea_feed.py` STOP list drops "chatgpt / claude / gemini / openai / google / meta / video / image…" as
   category words, and its name extractor only catches CamelCase/CAPS names. Big AI-tool news and most tool names
   ("Seedance", "Flow", "Muse") never surfaced as topics.
3. Fix (planned): daily mix rule (max 1 repo per day; at least 1 AI tool + 1 AI news) and a tool-aware extractor.
