# Why You Procrastinate — It’s Not Just Laziness

## Recommended title
**Why You Procrastinate — It’s Not Just Laziness**

## Description
You know the task matters. You may even care deeply about the outcome. So why does a smaller distraction keep winning?

This video explains the mechanism behind procrastination: present bias, emotional task cost, goal conflict, and the way your environment shapes the next action. The useful reframe is simple: procrastination is often less about laziness and more about avoiding an immediate emotional cost.

Instead of waiting for motivation, reduce the cost of starting. Make the next action visible, make it smaller than the emotion attached to it, and give yourself a short doorway into motion.

This is an educational explanation, not a diagnosis or a universal productivity prescription.
