**Title:** Turn Your Old Android Into a PC Control Panel | PhoneBox

**Description:**
Turn an old Android phone into a customizable computer control panel with PhoneBox, an open-source desk dashboard. See system stats, control media and volume, and access shortcuts from your phone.

PhoneBox uses an Android app and a desktop agent. Connect over USB or paired Wi-Fi, then choose and arrange widgets in the browser-based editor. Features and setup can vary by operating system and hardware; check the project's setup guide before installing.

PhoneBox: https://github.com/ersinkoc/phonebox
Setup guide: https://github.com/ersinkoc/phonebox/blob/main/docs/SETUP.md

Project screenshots show demo data; animated illustrations and the cover are conceptual. AI narration. PhoneBox is created by Ersin Koç and contributors; project assets used under the MIT License: https://github.com/ersinkoc/phonebox/blob/main/LICENSE
