# Reference study: gittrend.io (Instagram), 2026-10-24

Checked: 2026-10-24 13:10 UTC. Source: https://www.instagram.com/gittrend.io (public profile, read via the web profile endpoint).
Status in `research/sources.csv`: `manual` (the scanner does not read Instagram; this is a hand-kept reference).
Method: 12 most recent posts (captions, hashtags, views, likes, comments, dates), 6 videos downloaded and measured
with ffmpeg (duration, resolution, audio, scene changes), 1 contact sheet read by eye. Captions and hooks are quoted
only as observations. We do not copy wording, hashtags or layouts.

## Channel
- 173,025 followers, 648 posts, bio: "Find the next big open-source project / GitHub trends, every day".
- Every caption ends with the same promotional line ("More like this -> gittrend.io") and a cluster of 7 to 9 hashtags.
- Bio link points to their own promote page and a link-in-bio page.

## Delivery (what they do, observed)
- **Hook / caption:** the first sentence names the product and its outcome in plain words, often with one concrete number
  ("512 numbers", "154 tokens a second", "703 prompts", "1,800+ sites"). Qualifiers that matter to developers come early:
  free, open-source, runs locally, no bot, no upload.
- **Proof in the caption:** a specific claim a reader can check (a speed, a count, a feature), not adjectives.
- **Use prompt:** the caption tells the viewer what to do with it ("Copy one and try it with your own AI agent").
- **Format:** 720x1280 vertical, 30 to 45 s, original audio track present.
- **Media (contact sheet, OmniGet post):** README-style repo pages scrolled top to bottom, with large bold captions
  in the frame, a short cut every few seconds, and an occasional animated key-press or arrow. This is the repo-page
  delivery the owner has ruled out for our production. We learn the pacing and the caption size only.
- **Captions on screen overlap the page.** Our rule is that captions never cover media. Do not copy this.
- **Hashtags:** 7 to 9 per post, mostly generic (#opensource #github #developer #ai). Our rule is niche tags only and
  no #AI, #Coding or #Video. Use niche tags such as the product's own category.
- **Scene changes:** measured with ffmpeg at 0.05 threshold, the counts were 3 to 43 per clip. The count is noisy on
  motion graphics and text animation, so treat it as a rough indicator, not a target.

## What we take (learning, not copying)
1. Lead the caption with the outcome and one checkable number, then the qualifier that matters to the viewer.
2. Give the viewer one thing to try today, in our own words.
3. Pace: a clear moment every few seconds, and captions that stay clear of the media.
4. Pick products whose outcome can be shown in ten seconds of real product use, not a list of features.

## What we leave
- Repo-page scrolling as the main visual, promotional footers, and the link-in-caption format.
- Generic hashtag clusters. Our rules apply.
- Claims we cannot trace to a dated source. Their captions carry no sources; ours must.
