# 14 solo AI business ideas

These are business models to investigate, not guaranteed income. Pick a customer problem, validate demand, check costs and permissions, and test a small offer.

1. **AI team training:** teach a team how to use approved AI tools for a real workflow.
2. **Content services:** turn a client's ideas into social posts, with their review.
3. **Search visibility:** improve useful site content and technical search readiness; rankings aren't guaranteed.
4. **Ad creative testing:** produce and compare ad variations; platform rules, spend and results vary.
5. **Workflow automation:** connect repetitive tasks, with human checks where needed.
6. **Customer response support:** help draft or route customer answers; protect private data and escalate when appropriate.
7. **Ongoing service:** take responsibility for an agreed recurring deliverable, such as reporting.
8. **AI adoption advice:** audit workflows and recommend a realistic implementation plan.
9. **Client software:** build a focused application for one customer's problem.
10. **Audience and sponsorship:** publish useful content, build an audience and explore sponsorships.
11. **Digital products:** create and sell an original template, guide or other digital resource.
12. **Community or newsletter:** run a paid community or subscription newsletter.
13. **White-label software:** resell a product under your brand only where the provider's license allows it.
14. **Your own app:** build, test and offer a software product of your own.

AI output needs review. Costs, privacy obligations, licensing, demand and earnings vary. No earnings or business results are guaranteed.
