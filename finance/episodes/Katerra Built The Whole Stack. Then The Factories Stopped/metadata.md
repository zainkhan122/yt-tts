## DESCRIPTION
Katerra tried to own every step of a building — architect, factory, and jobsite — then the factories stopped in 2021.

The Menlo Park company, founded in 2015 by Michael Marks and Fritz Wolff, made walls with the windows already in them. Marks told CNBC there was no handoff. SoftBank's Vision Fund led $865 million in January 2018. The Information later put more than $2 billion into the stack. In October 2019 a San Diego hotel client told The Information the pitch was good and none of it arrived. On June 1, 2021, The Information reported Katerra had told staff it planned to shut down. On June 6 it filed Chapter 11 in Houston.

This film is the public record of that stack: the Phoenix plant that closed, the Tracy robots, the Spokane timber press, and the court sales that left the machines standing after the company did not.
