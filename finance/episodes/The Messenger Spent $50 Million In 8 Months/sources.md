## Villain (one)

They built a **prestige, multi-city, above-market newsroom** sized for **550 journalists** and **100 million** monthly readers, funded by **$50 million**, to live on **free-site digital ads** in **2023**. They took in about **$3 million**. Scale was the burn. Google/Meta are the weather (search/social ads). The cost structure is the villain.
