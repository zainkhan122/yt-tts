# Video 06 — What Happens When You Zone Out?

Viewer experience: noticing attention drift and realizing the mind is not switched off.
Primary mechanism: internally oriented thought, default-network participation, attention switching and task-set competition.
Counterpoint: mind-wandering can support planning and creativity; it is not automatically a deficit.
Guardrails: do not diagnose ADHD or any disorder; do not describe the default mode network as a single on/off switch; distinguish correlation from causation.
Visual treatment: landscape 16:9, one distinct visual per sentence beat, restrained selective kinetic emphasis only.
