# What a film has to *do* — not a clock

The 8-block spine is retired. A film still has jobs. Order comes from the record.

## Jobs

1. **Identify** — they know who it is in the first seconds (name + mark/object).
2. **Unfinished why** — one loop you will cash. Not a slogan from the last film.
3. **Promise** — why anyone signed up / paid / joined. Make the deal tempting.
4. **Machine** — how money was supposed to work. One mechanism.
5. **Crack** — the week the public could see it (sourced).
6. **Cash** — pay off the unfinished why. Dates. Who was left.
7. **Leave** — a last beat this film earned. Not the channel motto spoken again.

Skip a job only if the record doesn’t have it. Don’t pad origin to hit a timestamp.

## Attract

Browse: name they already feel + a picture of the death.  
Search: company in the first 5 title words + first description sentence.  
Shorts: one unfinished why, one mechanism, from that long.

## Keep

Picture change 4–6.5s, no loops.  
Captions 2–4 words on a spoken keyword.  
No mascot. No chart as the face. No karaoke.

## Never

Fiction. Fake mastheads. Fake app UIs. Spoken/on-screen legal.  
Identical intro or outro to a sibling. Listicle as the whole film.
