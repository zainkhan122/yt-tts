In April twenty twenty-two Convoy raised two hundred sixty million dollars at three point eight billion. Peak headcount about fifteen hundred. They said they expected a billion dollars of revenue.

Freight rates then fell. Headcount fell to about five hundred. On October eighteenth twenty twenty-three the load board went blank. Dan Lewis named a massive freight recession and a contraction in the capital markets.

The trucks were still rolling. They did not need that priced middle.
