In September twenty seventeen, the chief executive of Toys R Us put this in a bankruptcy filing. About four hundred million dollars a year, in cash, just to service the debt from a two thousand five buyout.

Kids were still buying toys. Amazon was still selling them. The cash was already promised to the lenders. Twelve years after Bain, KKR, and Vornado took the chain private, the American stores were paying the loan instead of the floor.

That is the unfinished why. The long film cashes it.
