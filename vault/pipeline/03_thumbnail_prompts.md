# 🖼️ SCROLL-STOPPER THUMBNAIL PROMPT SYSTEM
## For The Deeper Mind — psychology / rare personality niche

---

## THE FORMULA (Why This Works)

A scroll-stopper thumbnail in the psychology niche = **5 elements**:

| Element | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **1. One emotional focal point** | A face with intense emotion, or ONE striking symbol. Never more than one subject. |
| **2. High contrast** | Bright, well-lit subject against a dark/negative-space background. Dark thumbnails die in the feed. |
| **3. A curiosity gap** | Image + text together create an unanswered question: "What does she see?" "Is that mine?" |
| **4. Negative space for text** | 40-50% of the frame left clean (usually one side) so the words can breathe. |
| **5. Readable at 168×94 px** | Text ≤ 4-5 words, huge, with black outline. If it's unreadable at phone size, it's dead. |

**The 3-second test:** imagine the thumbnail at the size of a postage stamp next to 20 other thumbnails. Does it stop the eye? If the emotion + contrast + one word aren't instantly legible — redo it.

## THE MASTER PROMPT TEMPLATE (Fill in the brackets)

Paste this into Gemini / Imagen / Midjourney. **Always generate WITHOUT text** (AI text rendering is unreliable) — add text in Canva afterward (specs below).

```
Cinematic YouTube thumbnail, 16:9, 1280x720. [SUBJECT — e.g., "a young adult's face"]
with [EMOTION — e.g., "intense, vulnerable emotion, a single tear, eyes looking slightly off camera"].
[LIGHTING — e.g., "dramatic split lighting: warm golden key light on one side, soft cool blue fill on the other"],
high contrast, bright well-exposed midtones, crisp sharp focus, photorealistic.
[BACKGROUND — e.g., "clean dark charcoal background with empty negative space on the left side of the frame"].
Composition: subject on the right third, rule of thirds, space reserved on the left for text.
Moody cinematic color grade, deep shadows, shallow depth of field.
Highly detailed, 8K, professional photography style.
NO text, NO words, NO letters, NO logos, NO watermark, NO border.
```

## THE TEXT LAYER (Do This in Canva — Not AI)

After you generate the image, add text in Canva (Free):

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| **Words** | 2-4 max. The video's single most memorable line. |
| **Font** | Bold sans-serif (Montserrat / Archivo / Inter ExtraBold) |
| **Color** | White or gold `#E8C766` — never red (reads as clickbait, lowers CTR with this audience) |
| **Outline/stroke** | Black, 3-6 px — non-negotiable, makes text legible over ANY background |
| **Placement** | The negative-space side, bottom or top third |
| **Size** | Big enough to read at 15% zoom |

**Text formulas that convert in this niche:**
- The counterintuitive claim: `ISN'T YOURS` / `TOO AWARE` / `NOT BROKEN`
- The painful truth: `NOBODY GETS YOU` / `TOO DEEP`
- The challenge: `STOP FEELING LESS` / `FEEL IT ALL`

**Video #1 text (already on your thumbnail):**
- Small line: `MUCH OF WHAT YOU FEEL`
- Big line: `ISN'T YOURS`

## DO / DON'T

| ✅ DO | ❌ DON'T |
|---|---|
| One subject, one emotion | Busy collages, 3+ faces |
| Bright subject on dark bg | Dark subject on dark bg (invisible in feed) |
| ≤ 4 words, bold, outlined | Full sentences, thin fonts, no outline |
| Leave 40% negative space | Fill every corner |
| The image + text create a question | Image and text say the same thing |

---

## TEST YOURSELF BEFORE PUBLISHING

1. Zoom the thumbnail down to a tiny icon — can you still read the ONE key word?
2. Cover the text with your thumb — does the image alone still intrigue?
3. Would THIS viewer stop: someone doom-scrolling at 1am, feeling "different," half-asleep?

If yes to all three → publish it.
