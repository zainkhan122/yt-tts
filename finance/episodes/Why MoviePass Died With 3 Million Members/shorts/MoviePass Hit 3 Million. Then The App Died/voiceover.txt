This is MoviePass. Founded in New York in twenty eleven. Three million members by June twenty eighteen. Then the app went dark.

They sold a ticket for less than a ticket costs. Nine dollars and ninety five cents a month. One film a day. They paid the theater the full price.

The more people signed up, the faster it burned. By June twenty eighteen, more than three million people had joined the furnace. Dark in September twenty nineteen.
