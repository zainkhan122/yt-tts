## Description
You woke up tired. Again. And sleep didn't touch it — because the exhaustion
was never physical. It's the invisible labor of a mind that never stops
processing: rehearsing conversations that never happen, replaying ones that
did, reading every room, simulating every future.

In this video, we look at why deep thinkers (INFJ, INTJ, INFP, INTP) carry a
kind of fatigue no nap can fix — through Carl Jung's concept of psychic
energy: the mind runs on a finite budget, and your dominant introverted
function spends it all on directed thinking. The result is depletion that
looks like laziness from the outside and feels like arithmetic from the
inside.

We cover the Jungian mechanics (directed vs. fantasy thinking, the inferior
function as the unused rest-channel), the Maya experiment, why sleep and
vacations don't reach this drain — and five practical rules of energy
economics: externalize the loop, cap the rehearsal, feed the inferior
function, ration your depth, and build a real shutdown.

Your tiredness is not weakness. It's an invoice.
