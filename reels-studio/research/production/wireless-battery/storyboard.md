## Proposed narration

See your wireless battery levels right on your Windows taskbar. Meet Wireless Battery.

It brings supported mice, headsets, keyboards, and controllers into one glance, without opening a pile of vendor apps.

A red number warns you when a device hits fifteen percent or less. A blue bolt shows when it's charging.

Download the installer from the project's GitHub releases and run it. Check the supported device list first: not every model works.

It's free and open source. Comment BATTERY and follow Hypeless for more tools, minus the hype.
