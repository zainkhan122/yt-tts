# YouTube Short cover system

## Visual standard
Reference `approved-thumbnails/` and its user-approved manifest. Each Short has a distinct, topic-specific visual: a readable object or process from that tool's real subject. Use bright, clean color, visually substantial graphics, and a bold short outcome-led headline. Avoid generic reused icons and don't pretend illustration is a real product screenshot. The editorial safe bounds on a 1080 × 1920 canvas are x=54–1026, y=335–1610 for essential headline and artwork; inspect the 2:3 and 4:5 center crops, contrast, spelling, meaning, and mobile readability. These are internal editorial boundaries, not a platform display guarantee.

## Stages and truthful state
1. Inventory: `PYTHONPATH=. python3 tools/youtube/approved_cover_backlog.py` shows sent Shorts needing approved designs, pending approved files, blocked or confirmed. It covers tracked direct Shorts and prior Buffer Shorts; it is not a live census of untracked channel uploads.
2. Research each subject using the brief, saved publication text and original source. Prepare a distinct design brief and illustration. Artwork generation currently requires an interactive image-generation step; the cron has no image generator. `tools/cover_compose_approved_style.py` can place an authored headline onto an already-made illustration, but it neither generates art nor approves quality. Visually inspect crop bounds and contrast; no automatic QA claim.
3. Present each proposed cover for user approval. Only approved exact PNG files and digests go into `approved-thumbnails/manifest.json` and the corresponding named file in that directory. Verify the hash and safe crop before recording approval. The three current proposals in `covers/backlog/` are unapproved; do not put them in the manifest or publish them without approval.
4. The cover-specific Actions workflow runs four times a day and selects at most five approved, unconfirmed, digest-valid covers per run. Cover work is limited to the shared daily general-API budget minus 500 units, preserving that margin for Shorts metadata and scheduling; upload starts have a separate existing cap and cover calls do not consume it. Other API clients may consume project quota outside this journal, so the reserve is not a guarantee of available Google quota. It supports older tracked Shorts even if the active queue entry was retired, and tracked direct Shorts after their state is sent. It verifies YouTube public status, checks digest, writes a journal receipt, observes retry/blocked states, and emits a backlog report. GitHub cron timing is not guaranteed. Missing artworks are reported, not invented, automatically approved or silently called complete.
5. A confirmed receipt records successful image assignment and YouTube reporting that a custom thumbnail exists. It is not proof that every Shorts surface displays the exact image. Failures and quota deferrals remain pending or blocked; check the report and journal.

Existing scheduled videos and media are not replaced. New videos must separately meet the dedicated-cover/decoded-frame-zero contract before publication.
