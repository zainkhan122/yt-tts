## Description

Overthinking can feel like problem solving, but sometimes it is a loop wearing a lab coat. You replay the conversation, inspect every detail and keep searching for certainty.

This video explains how attention capture, threat appraisal, uncertainty and self-focused thinking can keep a thought loop running. It also separates useful reflection from repetitive analysis.

Reflection can help when it produces new information, a repair, a decision or a better next action. But if no new information would change what you do, another mental lap may only generate more emotional heat.

The practical exit is not to force the thought away. Ask what information would change your action, how you will get it and when the loop has reached its exit condition.
