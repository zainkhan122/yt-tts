# 📺 CHANNEL SETUP GUIDE — The Deeper Mind

Everything you need to launch the channel in ~10 minutes.

---

## 1. NEW ACCOUNT OR SAME EMAIL? → **New Google Account**

**Recommendation: Create a brand-new Google account for this channel.**

### Why a new account:
| Reason | Explanation |
|---|---|
| **Risk isolation** | Faceless + AI-assisted content carries real policy risk (YouTube's "repetitive content" and "inauthentic content" policies). If a channel is terminated for those violations, YouTube *can* take action on other channels on the same account engaged in the same behavior. A separate account protects your existing 3-4 channels. |
| **Resale value** | Faceless channels are commonly sold later (roughly $30-50 per 1,000 subscribers). A standalone account transfers cleanly. A channel buried in your personal account doesn't. |
| **Clean algorithmic signal** | YouTube associates account-level signals. A fresh account = a clean recommendation graph, no cross-links to unrelated channels. |

### What you DON'T lose by switching (important):
- **"Unlocked features"** (custom thumbnails, 15+ min videos, live streaming) → these are unlocked by **phone verification**, which takes 2 minutes on any new account.
- **AdSense approval** → monetization thresholds (1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours) are **per-channel** anyway. Your already-approved AdSense can be **linked to the new channel** when it's time. One AdSense = many channels.

### The plan:
1. Create new Gmail (e.g., `thedeepermind.yt@gmail.com`)
2. Create the channel → verify phone number
3. When monetized (months 6-12) → link your existing AdSense

> *Exception: if you never plan to sell and your other channels are completely unrelated, a new "Brand Channel" under the same account is acceptable. But the new account is strictly safer. I recommend the new account.*
