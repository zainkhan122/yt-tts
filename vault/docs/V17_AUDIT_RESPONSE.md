# Independent scoring of Video 017 against the August 31, 2026 channel audit

**Subject:** *The Psychology of the Friend Everyone Confides In* (v17, 8:01, shipped)  
**Audit corpus:** v1–v5 of the same channel (labeled in the PDF as “The Psychology of Emotional Absorption”). Overall channel score **78/100**. Visual editing scored **5.0/10 on every video**. Hooks **6.0–7.0**.  
**Method:** Same categories, same 2026 protocol the auditor used. No credit for “we meant to.” Spoken script, caption schedule, title, description, thumb, and both Shorts were checked against timestamps from `storyboard.json` / `final.mp4`.

This is not a rebuttal of the audit. Where v17 already fixed something, that is stated. Where it repeats the same failure, that is stated.

---

## 1. What the audit actually claims (channel-level, not optional)

The auditor’s verdict is one sentence: **scripts are ~9/10; YouTube is being treated like an audiobook.** Videos die in the 24–48h micro-test because:

1. **First 15–30s** delay the concept (Jung / mechanism / “what this is”). Relatability runs 60–90s.
2. **Visuals** are generic stock. No diagrams of the *mechanism*. Pattern-interrupt every 5–7s is claimed as needed; our beats are ~2.3–3s cuts of similar mood footage, which is not the same as a *conceptual* interrupt.
3. **Titles** are accurate-academic. Truncation on mobile. Curiosity/metaphor sits after “The Psychology of…”
4. **Ask YouTube / 2026 search** indexes *spoken* sentences more than tags. Conversational questions in-script matter more than hashtag count.
5. **Session contribution** (end-screens into the next cluster video) is missing.
6. **Shorts** should be 30–45s *loops* extracted from the highest-retention *mechanism* beat, not a compressed cold-open plus a subscribe tail.

Recurring strengths they already grant us (and v17 still has): prose, metaphor, comment CTAs. Those are not the bottleneck.

## 5. Visuals — the score that does not move until we change the pipeline

The auditor is not asking for “nicer stock.” They are asking for **one custom graphic per mechanism**, 12–20s, at the moment the mechanism is named.

For v17 the *missing* graphics (this is what a 6.5+ visual score would have required):

1. **0:00–0:20** — One-way valve: arrows in, none out. (We generated a brass valve still. It is not used as a *diagram* with labels.)
2. **2:51** — Mask vs face: “Listener” plate over a face, then lifted.
3. **4:04 inventory** — Five slots; each flips from “friend” to “appointment.”
4. **4:39 rent** — Three meters: exhaustion / language / resentment.

Until `pipeline.py` can overlay a titled diagram (even a static PNG we generate as 1 of the 10 image slots **reserved for graphics, not faces**), every future video will re-score **5.0 visuals**. That is not a taste issue. It is the stated #1 growth bottleneck.

Pattern interrupt: we already cut every sentence (~2–3s). That satisfies “something changes.” It does **not** satisfy “the viewer can *see the idea*.” Those are different.

## 8. Lessons to implement — only things that change the next video

Locked as production rules for **v18+** and Shorts. If a rule isn’t testable at `check_script.py` / storyboard, it will not survive.

### L1 — Hook clock (CRITICAL) — `check_script.py`

By **0:15** the script must contain: (a) the felt wound in one sentence, (b) the **named mechanism** (Jung or cognitive term, immediately anchored in plain English).  
By **0:30**: what they will be able to *do* or *name* by the end.  
Fail the script if the first topic-sentence or mechanism is after beat 8 (~20s at our cadence).

**Do not** open with a list of examples. Examples start after the mechanism is named.

### L2 — Title formula for Browse (HIGH)

Keep R11 rotation, but **the truncation line (first ~40 chars) must contain the curiosity or the wound**, not only “The Psychology of.”

Pattern: `[Metaphor or You-wound]. [Search phrase]`  
v18 (Black Sheep) should not ship as only “Why You Feel Like the Black Sheep of Your Family” without a browse variant in the pack: e.g. “They Love a Version of You That Isn’t Real.”

Pack always includes **Search / Browse / Hybrid** three titles. Hybrid is default unless user picks otherwise.

### L3 — One diagram slot per video (HIGH)

Of the 10 AI images, **reserve 2 for labeled diagrams** (mechanism + contrast), 16:9, high contrast, almost no photoreal clutter. Storyboard *forces* those assets onto the beat where the mechanism is spoken.

v18 minimum: family-psyche vs Self (two circles / one lamb vs flock — already in MONTH3 thumb direction).

This is the only way visual score leaves 5.0 without a new editor.

### L4 — Cut the “typical week” montage (MEDIUM)

If a section is a calendar of the same pain, cap it at **4 beats**. v17’s Mon–Fri dump is the over-venting they keep marking Medium. Inventory (questions) stays; lists of days go.

### L5 — Jargon anchor (MEDIUM)

Any Fe/Ni/persona/imago must be followed **in the same sentence or the next** by a physical metaphor. v17 did this well for persona (“the mask that makes you useful”) and badly for nothing — keep the same-sentence rule in `check_script`.

### L6 — Spoken Ask-YouTube questions (MEDIUM)

At least **two interrogative sentences in the first 12 beats** that a viewer might type into Ask YouTube. v17 has “who do you call?” — good, move it to beat 1–2.

### L7 — Session contribution (LOW–MEDIUM)

Last 20s of long-form: spoken handoff to **one named next video**, plus description line “Next: …”. End-screen when upload is manual: v16 cluster or v18. Metadata pack gets an `## END SCREEN` row.

### L8 — Shorts extraction (HIGH)

For each long-form, Shorts are **not** auto hook=beats 0–N.

- **Hook Short** = the single best *reframe sentence* in the first 2 minutes, looped, **≤35s including CTA**. Prefer cutting the CTA to a 0.8s text-only end card if completion % matters more than subscribe (test: payoff-style first).
- **Payoff Short** = self-contained mechanism (v17 inventory is the model). Never the closer / “one last thing.”
- Title of the Short = the reframe, not the long-form title.

### L9 — Do not inflate runtime to “look substantial”

8:01 is healthier than 11:00 under 2026 satisfaction weighting. v18 target **7–9 min**, ~130–160 beats, not 171 of listicles.

## 9. What we will *not* implement from the audit

Professional filter, not stubbornness:

- **Mirror-neuron / clinical neuroscience claims** we cannot source. The audit pushes “double-speed mirror neurons.” That is not our lane (R22 + safety). Jungian mechanism + felt metaphor stays. “Intellectual dopamine” = **named Jung + one physical metaphor by 0:15**, not fake lab numbers.
- **2D motion-graphics studio.** We don’t have it. Diagram stills (L3) are the feasible substitute.
- **Abandoning faceless/stock entirely.** Not required. They scored 5.0 for *generic* stock, not for faceless as such.
- **Keyword stuffing** they explicitly say is penalized. We already cut to 5 hashtags.

## 10. v18 acceptance bar (Black Sheep)

A v18 script is not “step a pass” unless:

1. Mechanism (family psyche vs Self) spoken by **0:15**.
2. Hybrid title in the pack; browse variant uses the *performance* wound (“They love a person who doesn’t exist”).
3. Two of ten images are diagrams (flock vs one; mask at the dinner table).
4. No 30s+ “typical Sunday dinner” montage before the mechanism.
5. Shorts plan named **before** long-form finalize: (A) costume-at-dinner reframe, (B) foreign-soil / tree metaphor payoff. Not beats 0–12 auto-hook.
