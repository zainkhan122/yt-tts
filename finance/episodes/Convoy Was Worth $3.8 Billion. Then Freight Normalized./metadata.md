## DESCRIPTION
Convoy cancelled every load on 18 October 2023. Eighteen months after a $3.8 billion price.

Dan Lewis and Grant Goodale, former Amazon, founded the Seattle digital freight brokerage in 2015. Press called it Uber for trucks. Gates and Bezos were on the cap table by 2017. CapitalG took them to a billion-dollar valuation in 2018. Generation and T. Rowe Price led $400 million at $2.75 billion in 2019. In April 2022 they raised $260 million at $3.8 billion — Baillie Gifford, T. Rowe Price, and $100 million of venture debt from Hercules, per FreightWaves — and said they expected to pass $1 billion of revenue.

Freight rates then fell. Headcount went from about 1,500 to about 500. On 18 October 2023 the load board went blank. On 19 October Lewis’s memo named a massive freight recession and a contraction in capital markets. GeekWire: last day for most; severance reportedly not paid. Flexport later bought the tech stack, not the company. Petersen called the price modest. The reported figure, unconfirmed, was $16 million.
