# Video 03 script — Why You Walk Into a Room and Forget Why

**Target:** 52 sentence beats · 6–7 minutes · one sentence equals one visual beat.

- pace: 0.92 | pause_after: 0.15s | emphasis: THE DOORWAY RESET

- narration: You walk into a room with a clear intention, and then the intention vanishes.

- narration: A second ago, you knew exactly what you needed.
