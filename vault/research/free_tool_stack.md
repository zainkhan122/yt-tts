## LAYER 4: "IMAGES TO VIDEO" — MAKING STILLS MOVE

You asked about "Google Flow" — I believe you mean using Google's tools to generate images then turning them into video. Here's the realistic answer:

**For 2026 psychology content, you DON'T need AI video generation (yet).** 

The proven format — used by Apex Psychology and virtually every successful faceless psychology channel — is:

```
Still images + subtle Ken Burns effect (slow zoom/pan) + text overlays + voiceover
```

This is done with:
1. Generate images via Google AI Studio/Imagen → save to your computer
2. Import into CapCut/DaVinci Resolve
3. Add slow zoom (2-5% scale over 8-15 seconds)
4. Layer text overlays, voiceover, and background music

**Why this works better than AI video:**
- AI-generated video clips (Kling, Runway, etc.) are still inconsistent — faces morph, scenes glitch
- Still images with Ken Burns effect look MORE professional for psychology content
- It's free (CapCut + Canva) vs. $10-20/month for AI video tools
- It's faster to produce
- The viewer is listening to the script, not watching the visuals — the images are emotional wallpaper

### When to Add AI Video (Later, When Revenue Justifies It):
- **Kling 3 Omni**: Best motion quality. 10 credits/day free (~2 clips). $10/mo paid.
- **Hailuo 2.3**: Unlimited slow queue, free. Watermark. Best budget option.

## THE REALITY CHECK:

The tool stack is NOT your bottleneck. Your bottleneck is:

1. **Script quality** — Is this insight genuinely new, or recycled from 100 other channels?
2. **Voice consistency** — One voice. Forever. No exceptions.
3. **Title + Thumbnail** — Without a curiosity gap, nothing else matters.
4. **Publishing consistency** — 3 videos/week minimum for the first 90 days.

A $0 tool stack with great scripts will outperform a $200/month tool stack with generic scripts. Every time.
