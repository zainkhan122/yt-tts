On March fifteenth, twenty eighteen, Toys R Us asked the court to liquidate seven hundred thirty-five United States stores. About thirty-three thousand people on that payroll. The last American floors went dark on June twenty-ninth.

Amazon was still selling the same toys. Walmart was still selling the same toys. What closed was a chain that had spent twelve years sending four hundred million dollars a year to a two thousand five loan. The specialty aisle moved. The debt was the reason.
