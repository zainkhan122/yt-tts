## DESCRIPTION
Circuit City opened 625 stores late on February 5, 2003. Stereophile reported the reason: about 3,900 commissioned salespeople, dismissed, to save about $130 million in payroll.

This is the public record of that floor. Wards Company, Richmond, 1949. Circuit City on the New York Stock Exchange, 1984. About 60,000 people at the 2000 peak. Then hourly. Then November 10, 2008, Chapter 11 in the Eastern District of Virginia. Then January 16, 2009, liquidate every store. More than 30,000 jobs in that liquidation. March 8, 2009, the original company was done.

Best Buy and the recession are in the obituaries. They are weather. The late opening is the mechanism.
