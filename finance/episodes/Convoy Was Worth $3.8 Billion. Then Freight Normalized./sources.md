# Sources — Convoy Was Worth $3.8 Billion. Then Freight Normalized.

Public record only. If it is not here, it does not go in the film.

## Company
- Convoy Inc., Seattle. Digital freight brokerage / trucking software: matched shippers with carriers (press shorthand: “Uber for trucks”). Wikipedia, *Convoy (company)*.
- Founded **2015** by **Dan Lewis** (CEO) and **Grant Goodale** (CTO), both former Amazon. Wikipedia; GeekWire (Soper, 1 Nov 2023).
- GeekWire Startup of the Year **2017**. Wikipedia citing GeekWire 4 May 2017.
- Not Convoy Logistics (Arkansas) or Convoy Systems (Kansas City). FreightWaves correction, 18 Oct 2023.

## Capital (the number)
- 25 Jul 2017: **$62 million** Series B led by Y Combinator Continuity Fund. New money included Cascade / **Bill Gates**, Mosaic, Bill Bradley, Barry Diller. Total raised then **~$80 million**. Existing names: Greylock, Marc Benioff, **Jeff Bezos** / Bezos Expeditions, others. Wikipedia citing Fortune, TechCrunch, Forbes.
- 2018: **$185 million** led by CapitalG at a **$1 billion** valuation. TechCrunch via Wikipedia.
- 13 Nov 2019: **$400 million** led by Generation Investment Management and T. Rowe Price at **$2.75 billion**. TechCrunch via Wikipedia.
- **April 2022:** **$260 million** Series E at **$3.8 billion** valuation. Existing investors Baillie Gifford and T. Rowe Price; **$100 million** of that round was venture debt from Hercules Capital. FreightWaves, Kingston, 18 Oct 2023. GeekWire, Soper, 19 Oct 2023: “just 18 months ago.” GeekWire 1 Nov 2023 also notes a **$150 million** J.P. Morgan line of credit at that time.
- April 2022: company said it expected to **surpass $1 billion in annual revenue**. GeekWire 19 Oct 2023.

## The weather (not a second villain)
- COVID-era truckload rates were elevated; 2022–2023 they fell. GeekWire 19 Oct 2023 citing Reuters (demand dip) and WSJ (revenue per truckload down); oversupply of trucking companies.
- Avery Vise, FTR: brokers squeezed because shippers can get rates from large asset-based carriers. Tim Denoyer, ACT Research: shippers moving loads onto private fleets. Craig Fuller, FreightWaves: “violent commoditized industry… one of its deepest recessions in decades and a sudden change in investor appetite from risk to unit economics.” GeekWire 19 Oct 2023.
- Do **not** treat “COVID” as the spine. The spine is building a late-stage unprofitable brokerage as if the spike were a climate.

## Shrink, then the empty board
- Peak headcount **about 1,500**. At shutdown **around 500**. GeekWire 19 Oct 2023.
- Layoffs: June 2022 (about 7%, shortly after the $260M raise); October 2022; February 2023 (Atlanta office closed); June 2023. GeekWire 19 Oct 2023.
- June 2023: Goodale steps down as CXO, advisory role. GeekWire; Wikipedia.
- August 2023: reports of a sale process / investment bank (FreightWaves: reportedly Goldman). Chapter, not spine. No deal.
- **18 Oct 2023:** Convoy cancels **all shipments**; load board blank. Customer note: “necessary steps to prepare Convoy’s business for a transition” in 24–48 hours; shippers could work with booked carriers directly. FreightWaves, Kingston/de León, 18 Oct 2023. **No bankruptcy filing** that day (FreightWaves public-records check).
- **19 Oct 2023:** core operations closed. Lewis memo to staff (GeekWire prints it). Last day for most. Laid-off staff **reportedly no severance**; stock options called worthless (Wikipedia citing contemporaneous press; GeekWire: “reportedly did not receive severance”).
- Lewis memo, quoted by GeekWire: “massive freight recession and a contraction in the capital markets”; “unprecedented freight market collapse”; “dramatic monetary tightening”; “We were running up the down escalator…. and it kept speeding up”; “perfect storm”; four months of strategic options that “none… materialized into anything sufficient.” Signed “#TruckYeah, Dan.”

## After (chapter, not spine)
- **1 Nov 2023:** Flexport acquires **technology stack**, not the company or liabilities. Petersen memo via GeekWire: “purchase price relative to value is modest.” **Reported $16 million** — FreightWaves later (28 Jul 2025): “reportedly $16 million, though neither company confirmed that price.” Attribute as reported. ~50 / “a few dozen” / “small group” of product/engineering kept. Lewis joined Flexport (left later). Wikipedia; GeekWire Soper 1 Nov 2023.
- Petersen (same memo): Convoy had “focused on driving immense scale by pursuing the biggest Fortune 500 FTL accounts. With that scale came complexity and burn… even with all of Convoy’s incredible tech, they were not able to reach the scale required to turn a profit.”
- Feb 2024: Flexport relaunches “Convoy Platform” as software for third-party brokers (not Convoy-the-broker). FreightWaves via Wikipedia.
- Jul 2025: DAT buys the platform from Flexport; reported near **$250 million**, companies declined to confirm. FreightWaves, Kingston, 28 Jul 2025. **Not this film’s ending.** The 2015–2023 broker died in October 2023.

## What is *not* the spine
- Bezos/Gates as villains. They wrote checks. The spike was the weather; the model was the bet.
- “Uber for trucks” as a punchline. That was the 2017 poster.
- Flexport’s own layoffs, Dave Clark, DAT flip — aftermath.
- No Chapter 11 at the death (do not invent a filing).
- Do not flatten reported $16M into an audited number.
