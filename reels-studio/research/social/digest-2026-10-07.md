# Social scan #1: lessons from AI-tool / GitHub-repo explainers (7 Oct 2026)

**Scope:** 11 videos from 6 accounts (9 TikTok, 2 YouTube Shorts). For each account: the top 2 by views from its latest 8. The method is `reels.py social`, which transcribes speech with whisper.cpp and pulls stats with yt-dlp.

**Not covered:**
- Instagram: it requires a login. These creators cross-post to Reels.
- Music-only videos with on-screen text: there's no speech to transcribe (OCR is a future add-on).

Full transcripts stay workspace-only. Only short hook excerpts are quoted here, for analysis.

## Lessons (each backed by the data above)

1. **The hook states a payoff or stakes in the first sentence. It never sets context.** The winners:
   - *stakes* ("if your country lost internet…", 378K);
   - *transformation* ("turns one illustration into an animated character", 59K);
   - *countdown promise* ("10 secret codes", 434K);
   - *"gold mine" + a number* (24.5K).
   
   Starting mid-tutorial ("If you go to the Extensions tab…") got the account's lowest views (8.3K).
2. **Name reveal by ~7 s.** "It's called Project Nomad" lands at 7 s, after the hook earns attention. The product name is never the first words, unless the name *is* the transformation.
3. **Feature beats every 4–5 s**, chained with "First… Then… You also get… There's also…". This is a list rhythm even in non-list videos.
4. **Pace: 160–190 wpm for explainers**, and up to 226 wpm for rapid lists. Our old Kokoro pace (×1.05 ≈ 150 wpm) is too slow, so the target is **~170 wpm**.
5. **One human opinion line wins likes.**
   - remy: "this feels less like a normal GitHub project and more like someone built a computer for the apocalypse", at 107 likes/1k, the best in the sample.
   - Our version is the honest verdict line ("The catch: …").
6. **A comment-keyword CTA multiplies comments 8–10×** (5.8–6.2 vs 0.5–0.9 per 1k). It works even in 23 s.
   - The others promise "I'll DM you the link", which needs DM automation we don't have.
   - Our honest version: **"Comment MOD and I'll pin the link."** We pin the link in the comments ourselves.
7. **A value close beats no close:** "And of course, the entire project is free and open source" (remy, both top videos).
8. **Length:**
   - repo spotlights: **23–41 s** (github.signals, zaindevx);
   - lists: **60 s** (sabrina, remy);
   - longer than 80 s only works with strong personality.
9. **Topics that travel:** utility + fear/curiosity (offline survival kit), creative tools (animate your art), and **gaming crossovers** (PS5 on PC). That supports universal-modder (AI mods games) as video #1.
10. **What we won't copy:**
    - "forbidden internet" piracy roundups (242K views, but they promote infringement). Now flagged by the radar's `piracy` rule;
    - fake "I'll DM you" promises;
    - "free API keys" bait.

## Applied to our pipeline (PIPELINE.md §4 updated)

| Rule | Old | New |
|---|---|---|
| Speaking pace | ×1.05 (~150 wpm) | **~170 wpm**, speed calibrated per voice (`research/voice-audition/`) |
| Spotlight length | 45 s | **35–42 s** |
| Name reveal | in the hook | **by ~7 s, after the payoff hook** |
| Beat length | free | **one feature / visual change every 4–5 s** |
| Opinion line | none | **1 honest verdict line** ("The catch…" / "Honestly…") |
| CTA | question + follow | **"Comment KEYWORD and I'll pin the link"** + follow |
| Close | CTA only | **value close** ("free and open source") before the CTA |

**Next scan:** monthly, plus whenever a new format appears. Add 3 more accounts per scan to grow the sample. The current n = 11 is directional, not statistical.
