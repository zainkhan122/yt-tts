## DESCRIPTION
Quibi raised $1.75 billion. It lasted six months.

Founded in Los Angeles in August 2018 as NewTV by Jeffrey Katzenberg, with Meg Whitman as CEO, the company renamed itself Quibi — quick bites — in October 2018. It launched on 6 April 2020 as a mobile, short-form subscription: episodes of ten minutes or less, $4.99 a month with ads or $7.99 without, and a 90-day free trial around launch. Hollywood studios had put up the first billion. The app was phone-only for months. YouTube and TikTok were already on that phone, free.

On 21 October 2020 Katzenberg and Whitman announced a wind-down. The last day of service was 1 December 2020. Contemporaneous reports put subscribers around 500,000. Quibi did not own its shows; it licensed them. In January 2021 Roku bought rights to 75 programs for less than $100 million and put them on a free, ad-supported channel.
