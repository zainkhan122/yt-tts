# Video 05 brief — Why You Procrastinate (It’s Not Just Laziness)

## Viewer experience
The viewer knows a task matters but chooses a smaller, easier action because the immediate emotional relief beats the distant reward.

## Promise
Explain procrastination as a time-and-emotion problem rather than a moral identity.

## Mechanism
Present bias, task aversion, mood repair, attentional control and goal conflict.

## Counterpoint
Workload, sleep, environment, mental health and executive-function differences matter. Do not claim one brain circuit or one trick explains everyone.

## Visual thesis
A person stands between a distant valuable goal and a nearby glowing relief button. Each avoidance choice feels rewarding now while moving the goal farther away.

## Voice direction
Empathetic, concrete and energetic. Target 140–145 WPM.

## Short candidates
- Hook: “Procrastination is often a mood decision disguised as a time decision.”
- Payoff: “Make the next action smaller, visible and immediate.”
