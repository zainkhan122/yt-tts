# 📅 30-DAY PLAN — The First Month of The Deeper Mind

## Upload Rate — My Honest Recommendation

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| 1 video per day? | **Not at first.** Start at 3/week, ramp to 5/week, go daily only in Month 2+ if quality holds. |
| More than 1/day? | **Never.** It cannibalizes your own views, signals spam to the algorithm, and quality collapses. |
| Why not daily from day 1? | You're still learning the pipeline. 3 polished videos beat 7 rushed ones — YouTube judges your channel on **average CTR and retention**, not volume. |
| Shorts? | **Yes — 1 Short per long video.** Repurposed 30-60s clips. They're your fastest path to the 1,000-subscriber threshold. |

### The ramp schedule:
- **Days 1-7:** 3 long-form + 3 Shorts (build the muscle)
- **Days 8-14:** 4 long-form + 4 Shorts (pipeline is smooth)
- **Days 15-21:** 5 long-form + 5 Shorts (full speed)
- **Days 22-30:** 6-7 long-form + 6-7 Shorts (near-daily)

**Total Month 1: ~19-20 long-form videos + ~18 Shorts.** That's aggressive but achievable with the pipeline above (~25 min of your time per video).

---

## THE 4 PHASES (This is the psychological strategy, not just a list)

1. **Phase A — "Make Them Feel Seen"** (validation → early subscribes)
2. **Phase B — "Explain the Why"** (Jungian frameworks → watch time)
3. **Phase C — "Challenge Them"** (shadow work → comments + shares)
4. **Phase D — "Give Them Tools"** (integration → return viewers)

## SHORTS PLAN (1 per long video)

For each long video, cut the single most powerful 30-45 second moment:
- Vertical 9:16 format
- Same voice, same visual style
- Caption-heavy (most Shorts are watched on mute)
- End with the full video linked

**Short title examples:**
- "Why you feel everything so deeply 🧠 #psychology #infj"
- "3AM overthinking explained in 40 seconds"
- "Jung's darkest insight #shadowwork"
