# Video 04 brief — The Mechanism Behind Overthinking

## Viewer experience
The viewer replays a conversation or decision, believing another mental lap will create certainty, but the loop only produces more tension.

## Promise
Separate useful problem solving from repetitive self-focused thought and show why the loop feels productive.

## Mechanism
Attention capture, threat and value appraisal, uncertainty, repetitive negative thinking and action conflict. The loop is maintained when thought generates emotion but no new decision-relevant information.

## Counterpoint
Reflection can be adaptive. This video does not diagnose rumination or reduce it to one brain network.

## Visual thesis
A hallway loop repeats the same scene; each lap adds annotations and emotional heat but no new exit. A real exit appears only when a question changes the next action.

## Voice direction
Warm, grounded and increasingly urgent, then calm at the exit. Target 140–145 WPM.

## Short candidates
- Hook: “The loop feels like progress because every lap contains a new detail.”
- Payoff: “Ask what information would change your action.”
