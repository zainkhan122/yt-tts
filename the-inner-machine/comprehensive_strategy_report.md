14. **Transition Frequency Standard [OBSERVED FACT]**: Rapidly growing emerging channels like RAOUL Explains keep visual transitions fast (every 3-4 seconds) to cater to the shorter attention spans of younger digital natives [9].
15. **The Long-Form to Short-Form Conversion Gimmick [HYPOTHESIS]**: Extracting standalone philosophical hooks or shocking facts from long videos and re-editing them into highly rhythmic Shorts creates a reliable, organic subscriber-acquisition funnel without requiring independent Shorts production [37, 38, 39].

*   **Emotional Impact (1–10)**: Does the statement make the viewer feel deeply validated or introspective? [39]

#### Extraction Workflow

3. **The Re-Editing Rule**: Never post raw long-form footage. Crop to a vertical 9:16 aspect ratio, use highly readable, clean sans-serif dynamic captions in the center, and add soft lofi sound design to keep pacing fast and engaging [31, 37].
