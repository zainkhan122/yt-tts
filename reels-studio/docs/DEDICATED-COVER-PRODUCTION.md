# Dedicated covers: mandatory Short production

Each new Short gets purpose-made artwork and an original, mobile-readable cover, not an arbitrary frame pulled from the video or an old reusable template. Compose the cover in the approved style with headline and safe crop padding. Embed that cover as frame zero of the final MP4; preserve the same cover image in the pinned kit for YouTube. Frame zero is an editorial placement, not a guarantee of which frame every platform displays.

The artist or production reviewer inspects the exact finished artwork and video, and runs `tools/certify_dedicated_cover.py --id VIDEO-ID --video FINAL.mp4 --cover COVER.jpg --art PURPOSE-MADE-ART.png --headline 'HEADLINE' --confirm-purpose-made`. It checks 1080x1920, decodes the video's first frame, measures pixel difference against the actual cover (lossy compression tolerance), and pins video, cover and artwork hashes. The purpose-made origin is an honest human/production attestation: automated checks do not determine creative quality or prove ownership. Do not generate a receipt for extracted footage masquerading as designed art.

`dedicated_cover_gate.py` fails closed for missing, malformed, mismatched, reused, or non-frame-zero receipts in queue approval, new Buffer delivery, Buffer media substitution, and direct YouTube Shorts upload/scheduling, including pilot paths. Different videos cannot share the same artwork digest. Existing schedules and previously confirmed covers are not replaced or retroactively changed. These managed-workflow checks cannot prevent manual uploads outside the system.

The compositor/certifier and gate do not generate art independently: a production operator must create the image using the available image-generation/design tool, then follow the certificate workflow. The workflow blocks delivery if that step was skipped. YouTube thumbnail application must additionally be confirmed by provider readback; presence is not proof of exact pixels or of Shorts-shelf display.

## Render-stage enforcement

The standard `reels.py make` / cloud production renderer requires the graphics path, a Cover Lab selection and separate purpose-made source art before a video render. After creating original art specifically for the Short and composing/selecting its cover, put the art in `covers/VIDEO-ID/` and add these fields to that folder's `selection.json`: `"purpose_made_art": "art.png"`, `"art_sha256": "sha256:<actual SHA-256 of art.png>"`, and `"art_origin": "purpose-made-for-this-video"`. This is an honest operator attestation, not an automated origin or quality judgment. Pack selection, cover and art together with `cover_store.py`; do not use an extracted frame as art. The pipeline embeds the selected cover into the output and verifies frame zero.

The `one_go_review.py` custom review entry point requires separate source art and cover before dispatching voice, requires a fresh declared video output, verifies the shared narrated-outro assembly marker, then embeds and verifies the cover in that output. After inspection, issue the separate cover and outro receipts for the finished video. An assembly marker is not an audiovisual review, owner approval or YouTube thumbnail application. Direct invocation of arbitrary scripts and manual uploads can bypass production entry points; managed social delivery still requires the hash-bound cover and outro receipts. Existing provider schedules are not modified.
