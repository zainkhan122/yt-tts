# Muse Spark Open (Meta) — fact sheet (checked 2026-10-16)
Sources: Meta announcement via TechCrunch 2026-10-15 + AI at Meta post (816B/42B, 1M ctx) + press (Times of India).
- 816B total / 42B active parameters, 1M-token context, natively multimodal (text/image/video/audio); built for coding and long agentic work. Meta calls it the most capable US open-weight model (vendor claim).
- Access: APIs (Baseten, Fireworks) or downloadable weights under the Muse Model License (free for many developers, limited commercial terms — never call it Apache/unrestricted).
- Catch (mandatory, press-reported): local runs need ~1 TB RAM; two Mac Studios with M5 Ultra chips. It does NOT run on a normal Mac.
