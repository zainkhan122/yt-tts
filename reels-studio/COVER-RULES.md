# Cover rules (owner, 2 Nov 2026)

Every Short gets a designed cover. It is the first frame every platform shows, so it has to make a
viewer stop, read the headline, and want the answer. These rules apply to every cover from now on.

## 1. Headline (the words on the cover)

- **4 to 5 words**, all caps, bold, at the top of the cover.
- **Click-baited and intent-seeking.** It must do one of these, and must be true to the tool:
  - ask the viewer's real question ("CAN AI RUN YOUR PC?"),
  - promise a concrete outcome ("GET YOUR GUIDE TO INSTALL"),
  - or open a specific, checkable gap ("NOW AI TO RUN YOUR PC").
- **Honest.** No invented numbers, no "free" or "best" unless the repo shows it, no claim the video
  cannot prove. Click-bait that the video does not pay off is a failure, not a win.
- Plain words. No hashtags, no emoji, no punctuation other than "?" or "!" where it reads naturally.

## 2. Graphic (the visual that sits with the headline)

The graphic must be **highly related to the tool**: what it does, shown in its own world.

- **First choice: the tool's own media.** Logo, chart, diagram, screenshot, or demo frame from the
  repo or its README. No licence requirement. It can be used if it is:
  - sharp at cover size (at least 1080 px wide on its longest side, no visible blur or heavy compression),
  - readable at phone size (the tool's name or main subject is clear without zoom),
  - not cluttered with unrelated text or other products.
- **Fallback: a designed graphic built from the tool's world.** Use this when the tool's own media
  is missing, low quality, or unclear. Examples of the composition:
  - a laptop with a few windows open (browser, code editor, video editor, terminal) and the tool's
    agent or logo drawn on the left or right side.
  - a hand-built diagram of the tool's flow (input → agent → result).
  The fallback must be drawn from what the tool actually does, not a generic AI picture.
- The graphic is the main visual. Generated text cards are never the main visual.

## 3. Layout

- Canvas 1080 × 1920. Headline in the top band (safe zone starts at y = 330).
- Graphic in the middle or lower half, large, not cut off by the platform UI.
- Keep the bottom 330 px clear of anything important (platform buttons).
- Three concepts per video, each using a different layout:
  - **bleed**: graphic full-bleed behind the headline,
  - **window**: graphic inside a framed window or laptop,
  - **split**: headline and graphic side by side or top and bottom.

## 4. Contrast and legibility

- Headline contrast against its actual background: at least **4.5:1** (measured, not guessed).
- Maximum three colours plus white and black. One accent colour, used for the key word or underline.
- Headline must not overlap the graphic's key detail.

## 5. Selection

- Each video gets **three concepts**: different headline or different layout, same tool, same truth.
- The viewer panel judges the three cold. The winner is pinned as the cover.
- A set that is the same design three times fails.

## 6. What fails (automatic reject)

- Headline outside 4–5 words, or not all caps.
- A headline that promises something the video does not show.
- A generated graphic that could be any AI tool (not tied to this tool's world).
- A graphic that is blurred, pixelated, or unreadable at phone size.
- Any text under contrast 4.5:1.
- Only one layout across the three concepts.
