## DESCRIPTION
Net worth is the scoreboard. Salary is a flow that can vanish. This video shows three net worth numbers that matter before 40, drawn as gauges, not as shame. First: above zero — solvency. If assets minus debts is negative, a raise is not a plan; the hole is. Second: a buffer measured in months of essential costs, commonly taught as about three months, which we treat as a teaching mark, not a law. Third: optionality — a net worth that can buy time, often discussed as about one year of gross salary, again a mark, not a prescription. We use the Federal Reserve Survey of Consumer Finances so you can see where US medians sit by age, and we say out loud that medians are not goals. The point of the graphic is the difference between a flow and a stock. People optimize the flow because it shows up every two weeks. The stock is what lets you survive a gap in the flow. Education, not financial advice. Your cost of living is not the example on screen. Comment which of the three gauges you can actually fill in without guessing.
