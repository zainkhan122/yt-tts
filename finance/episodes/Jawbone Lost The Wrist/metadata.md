## DESCRIPTION
Jawbone sat out Christmas 2014 with the UP3. In July 2017 the company was liquidated.

AliphCom, founded in San Francisco in 1999 by Alexander Asseily and Hosain Rahman, made a DARPA-backed noise-cancelling headset, then the Jawbone Bluetooth, then Jambox, then the UP band. Fortune, in January 2015, had them at more than $400 million raised, a valuation above $3 billion, about 450 people — and still not profitable. The first UP malfunctioned; Jawbone offered full refunds. The UP3 missed Christmas 2014 on a production glitch.

The Verge, 27 May 2016, confirmed UP production had stopped and leftover bands were sold off. TechCrunch, 6 July 2017, confirmed liquidation (Sherwood Partners) as Rahman moved to Jawbone Health Hub, a new company. PitchBook via TechCrunch: around $951 million raised. Fitbit and Apple Watch were still on wrists. The consumer band was not.
