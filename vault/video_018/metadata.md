## LONG-FORM DESCRIPTION

Why you're the black sheep of your family is not a character flaw. At the table you perform a version of you nobody knows. They love a person who does not exist. Carl Jung called that assignment the family psyche — a weather system you were born inside — and the Self that arrived later with its own instructions.

If you are INFJ, INTJ, INFP, or INTP, you felt foreign in the house that raised you. Intuition saw the pattern. Feeling carried moods that were never yours. Thinking asked why, and why is dangerous at a table that runs on because I said so. Individuation is not cruelty. It is the tree that grew from different soil still standing in the old orchard.

In this video: the costume of a normal week, the three family parts (golden, problem, quiet), the price of two selves, and four small ways to stop performing without a door slam — one true sentence outside the house, one thing you will not fake, letting them be confused, and finding the other trees.
