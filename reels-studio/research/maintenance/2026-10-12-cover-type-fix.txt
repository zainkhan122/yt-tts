2026-10-12 — mobile-safe cover title typography

Owner report: newer video covers had too-small title type compared with the initial clips. Compared
reference previews `renders/covers/news-claude-free-01-cover.jpg` and `tool-muse-01-cover.jpg`
(large, bold, two-line clip hook) against image-hook previews `tool-qwen38-01-cover.jpg` and
`repo-ds4-01-cover.jpg` (single-line title rendered much smaller).

Root cause
- Clip hooks use `.sp-headline`, `A.fit(max=200, min=80, maxH=380)` and a 380 px title band.
- Image/page/stat/steps/terminal hooks use the general `.sp-heading`, `A.fit(max=128, min=56, maxH=180)`.
- Long image-hook titles therefore shrink to a 56 px one-line style, much less readable on mobile.

Fix
- The shared heading builder now detects the first scene (`instant`) and gives that hook the
  `cover-heading` class: same 200/80/380 fitting range and the same 380 px title band as the original
  clip hooks. Later in-video headings keep the old smaller layout and fade-in.
- Reflowed first-scene supporting visuals below the title band: image subtitle at y=616, image card
  at y=700 (dynamically moved down for a multi-line subtitle); browser cards at y=700; terminal/stat
  cards at y=700; first steps card at y=900.
- `tests/test_frame0_cover.py` exercises the real scene builder in Node and asserts first-scene font
  limits, scene/cover distinction, and image-card layout with and without subtitles.

Validation: frame-0 suite = 14 tests OK; full suite = **322 tests OK**; `node --check`
`templates/_spotlight/scene.js` passes.

Scope decision (owner, 2026-10-12): **future renders only**. No current Release, Buffer queue entry,
scheduled slot or sent post was changed. Nine currently scheduled image-hook videos (24 Buffer rows)
retain their small-font frames. The prior no-rebuild wave-2 scope remains honored; any later rollout
would need an explicit scope change, then re-render/re-pin/in-place Buffer refresh. Sent posts cannot
be fixed by refresh.
