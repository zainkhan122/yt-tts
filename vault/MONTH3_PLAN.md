### V17 — The Psychology of the Friend Everyone Confides In ⭐
- **Felt pain:** "Everyone dumps their secrets on me. But when I'm drowning,
  there's nobody to call. I'm everyone's therapist; who's mine?"
- **Mechanism:** the Helper PERSONA (from v12, now its own video) + dominant
  Fe built to receive others' processing but never taught to hand any back —
  the one-way valve of extraverted feeling.
- **Register check:** fresh (v15 = pushing help away while hurting; this =
  the role asymmetry of always being the listener).
- **Demand:** "therapist friend" / "everyone tells me their problems" —
  evergreen, heavily memed by exactly our audience. Massive comment-bait
  ("what's the heaviest thing someone dumped on you?").
- **Thumbnail direction:** an empty armchair facing a full waiting room.

### V18 — Why You Feel Like the Black Sheep of Your Family
- **Felt pain:** "At family dinners I perform a version of me nobody knows.
  They love a person who doesn't exist."
- **Mechanism:** differentiation from the FAMILY PSYCHE — the collective
  persona a family builds for you at birth vs the Self that arrives with its
  own instructions (Jung: the tree that grew from foreign soil).
- **Register check:** fresh (v10 = cost of authenticity in general; this =
  the family-of-origin specifically, the first audience you ever masked for).
- **Demand:** "black sheep of the family" — evergreen phrase, family-of-origin
  content is a proven top-performing psych sub-niche.
- **Thumbnail direction:** one lamb standing opposite a flock (symbol, not face).

### V19 — The Psychology of Grieving Someone Who's Still Alive
- **Felt pain:** "I already miss them, and they're sitting right there. I
  finished the relationship in my head before it ended."
- **Mechanism:** the IMAGO — you love and grieve the inner image, not the
  person; when the person stops matching the image, Ni completes the pattern
  early and mourning begins while they're still in the room.
- **Register check:** fresh (v8 = chasing unavailable partners; this =
  mourning the available ones you've internally outgrown; v11's foresight
  applied to endings).
- **Demand:** "grieving someone still alive" — large evergreen search phrase,
  almost no Jungian treatment exists. Emotional, comment-driving.
- **Thumbnail direction:** two chairs, one occupied, one holding a folded photo.

### V20 — You See Every Possible Outcome — and Still Can't Decide
- **Felt pain:** "I can argue every option into a win and a disaster. So I
  choose nothing. Then blame myself for the delay."
- **Mechanism:** Ni's branching futures vs inferior Se's present-tense action
  — foresight as paralysis; the simulation can't converge because every
  branch is REAL to the simulator.
- **Register check:** fresh (v6 = anxious loop mechanics at night; this =
  decision paralysis as foresight's cost, daytime, choice-centered).
- **Demand:** "decision paralysis" + "overthinking decisions" — v6 proved the
  in-niche appetite; this is its unclaimed sibling.
- **Thumbnail direction:** a figure at a fork of six identical doors.

### V21 — Why You Keep Outgrowing the People Around You
- **Felt pain:** "The friends who saved me at 19 have nothing to say to me at
  31. I feel guilty — and lonely at a new altitude."
- **Mechanism:** INDIVIDUATION DIVERGENCE — two people entering the process at
  different depths; growth is not betrayal, but it IS a filter (Jung: the
  people meant for who you were vs who you're becoming).
- **Register check:** conditional-fresh (v10 = loneliness while becoming;
  this = the specific MECHANICS of relationships expiring as you grow — must
  center the relationships, not the loneliness).
- **Demand:** "outgrowing friends" — evergreen, extremely shareable.
- **Thumbnail direction:** two figures climbing, one higher on the ridge line.

### V22 — The Psychology of Feeling Detached From Your Own Life
- **Felt pain:** "I narrate my life instead of living it. Like watching my
  own movie from the back row."
- **Mechanism:** the OBSERVER-SELF — Ni's permanent zoom-out vs inferior Se's
  embodiment; you process life as material. Coming back into the scene.
- **Register check:** fresh (no register row touches detachment; v16's
  "tab still open" was energy, not embodiment). FENCE NOTE: experiential
  Jungian framing only — never clinical dissociation claims (R22 + safety).
- **Demand:** "feeling detached from my life" / "watching life from outside" —
  widespread, mostly answered clinically; our felt-experience angle is open.
- **Thumbnail direction:** a figure watching their own silhouette on a screen.
